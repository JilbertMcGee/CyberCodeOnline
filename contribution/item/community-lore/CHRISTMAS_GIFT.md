After several wars and many hard yearsof crime the public forgot about most holidays, including Christmas. Santa was now just the name of various lowlife wannabe Spanish gang members and meant nothing other than stay away or get robbed and killed. Eventually megacorporations got their greedy little hands into some pre-war files about Christmas and discovered how profitable it was, even the old corporations could use it for monetary gain. They created the CyberSanta line of tech and gifts, and put them all in little boxes that costed upwards of 5000 bitcoin each. As always, some rebellion group thought they'd be the ones in thehistory books for blowing up the factories that made the CyberSanta gifts, and raiders had their own little Christmas with the leftovers, now they sit in dungeons under rubble, forgotten about or stashed away for next Christmas so thugs believe they actually can give their kids a life that they're happy about.
